## Notes on sources

- All Danish government sources are official publications. Parliamentary debate transcripts are verbatim records.
- The ILGA-Europe Rainbow Map is an annual benchmarking tool covering 49 European countries. It measures laws and policies, not individual decisions or outcomes.
- UNHCR Guideline No. 9 is the primary international standard for SOGIESC-based asylum claims. It is included in the Refugee Appeals Board's own background-material library.
- The EUAA SOGIESC guide (2024) is the most recent EU-level procedural guidance for this category of claims.
- The Hertz & Jarlner (2025) study is a peer-reviewed academic publication analysing approximately 15,000 anonymised Refugee Appeals Board case summaries.
