# Denmark SOGIESC Asylum Reform — Public Policy Documentation

This repository contains the source documentation, fact-check note, and primary source index for the public policy campaign:

**"Equality cannot stop at Denmark's border"**

The campaign calls on the Danish government and Parliament to introduce enforceable SOGIESC (sexual orientation, gender identity, gender expression and sex characteristics) procedural safeguards into Danish asylum law and third-country-transfer legislation.

## The policy proposal in brief

The campaign asks Parliament and the government to:

1. Place enforceable SOGIESC interview and credibility safeguards in the Aliens Act or binding regulations
2. Prohibit credibility findings based on appearance, mannerisms, or a single expected coming-out narrative
3. Require independent recurring audits of anonymised LGBTQIA+ asylum decisions
4. Mandate specialist training for caseworkers, board members and interpreters
5. Publish anonymised outcome and quality-review data sufficient for public oversight
6. Write explicit SOGIESC safeguards into third-country-transfer law before any transfer takes place

---

## What this repository is not

This is a public policy resource. It does not concern any individual asylum case. It does not allege that the Prime Minister personally directs individual decisions, that every rejection is wrong, or that LGBTQIA+ identity automatically creates a right to asylum.
